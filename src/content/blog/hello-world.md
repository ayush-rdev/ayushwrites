It's traditional to start a blog with a hello post, and I'm nothing if not traditional.

**Why this exists.** I've been meaning to have a place of my own on the internet for a while now. Somewhere without feeds, without likes, without an algorithm deciding what I see. Just a quiet corner where I can write things down and leave them for whoever wanders by.

**What to expect.** Notes on what I'm building, what I'm reading, and whatever half-formed thoughts survive long enough to be typed out. Nothing scheduled, nothing optimized. Posts when I feel like it.

> a blog is a conversation you have with the future you.

That's it. That's the whole plan. Welcome.
